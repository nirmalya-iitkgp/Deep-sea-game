**DEEP SEAS** is a high-stakes, deep-sea strategy game where you manage an abyssal base. Explore the dark depths, reveal forgotten sectors, and connect vital power vents while battling the encroaching entropy of cellular automata.

---

## 🎮 Core Mechanics

### 🌀 Exploration (Drunkard's Walk)
The abyss is dark. Use your **Sonar Pulse** (C-Key or Click) to reveal hidden sectors. Each pulse consumes energy but lights up the world using a stochastic walk algorithm, ensuring every mission feels unique.

### 🕸️ Connectivity (Minimum Spanning Tree)
Cables are expensive. The base automatically calculates the most efficient way to link your network. You only pay for the connection to the nearest existing link, encouraging smart, compact base expansion.

### 🦠 Environmental Entropy (Cellular Automata)
The ocean floor is alive. Heat vents and Abyssal Cracks evolve in real-time according to specific rulesets:
- **Still-life:** Stable patterns (The Shallows)
- **Oscillators:** Pulsing hazards (Thermal Ridge)
- **Spaceships:** Moving threats (Abyssal Trench)

---

## 💎 Rewards & Ranks

### 🏺 Ancient Relics
Hidden in the deepest trenches are ancient relics. Finding them grants a massive **+50 Energy Boost** and counts toward your final mission completion score.

### 🏆 Ranking System
Your performance is graded based on **Energy Efficiency**:
- **S-Rank:** >80% Energy Retained
- **A-Rank:** >50% Energy Retained
- **B-Rank:** >30% Energy Retained
- **C-Rank:** Survival Only

---

## 🛠️ Controls

- **DRAG & DROP (BUILD):** Connect revealed sectors to your power grid.
- **W-KEY (WELD):** Enter repair mode. Click broken (magenta) cables to fix them.
- **C-KEY (SONAR):** Release a sonar pulse at the cursor position.
- **M-KEY / MENU:** Return to the level selection deck.
